DEHORN

About

Dehorn is an industrial / progressive metal project created, composed, and engineered by Chris Clement — guitarist of SWARMER, former bassist of GAZA, and former guitarist of Bird Eater, Day of Less, Pilot This Plane Down, and Team Dead.

The music is heavy, synth-driven, and cinematic. Crushing guitar riffs locked into pulsating rhythms, layered over samples pulled from internet oddities and public domain cult films. The narratives running through it — religion, political collapse, societal decay — aren’t subtexts. They’re the whole point. Visceral and immersive, Dehorn sounds like it was built for the end of the world: equally at home on a festival stage, a film score, or a video game soundtrack.

Sound

Heavy guitar-driven riffs meet synth-heavy atmospheres and pulsating beats — a soundscape built around rhythm, texture, and emotional storytelling. Sound samples from haunting internet oddities and public domain cult films weave thematic narratives through the static of religion, politics, and societal collapse.

Live

Dehorn’s live show is a fully synchronized audiovisual production. Precision lighting, timed visuals, and a locked-in performance that puts the audience inside the music.
